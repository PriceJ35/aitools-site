What Is ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs is an AI voice synthesis platform that generates hyper-realistic speech from text. It supports voice cloning, multilingual generation across 32 languages, and fine-grained control over emotion, pacing, and tone. Used by content creators, publishers, game developers, and enterprises for everything from audiobooks to customer service.
