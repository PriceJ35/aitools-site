What Is Notion AI?

Notion AI is the AI-powered layer built into Notion, the all-in-one workspace app. It provides writing assistance, content summarization, task automation, and database insights directly within your notes, docs, and project boards. Unlike standalone AI tools, Notion AI works within your existing workspace context, making it ideal for teams who already use Notion for project management and documentation.

In-Article Ad - 336x280

Key Features

  • Inline Writing AI — Improve, translate, simplify, or rewrite any text directly in Notion
  • Page Summarization — Instantly summarize long docs, meeting notes, and databases
  • Q&A Chat — Ask questions about your entire workspace and get AI-powered answers
  • Autofill Database — Automatically populate database properties based on content
  • Meeting Notes — Generate action items, summaries, and follow-ups from notes
  • Translation — Translate content across 15+ languages while maintaining formatting

Our Verdict: 4.4/5 — Best for Teams

Notion AI is the best choice for teams already using Notion who want AI baked into their workflow. The seamless integration and workspace Q&A feature make it incredibly useful for daily productivity. For individual power users who prioritize note-linking, Obsidian is worth considering.
