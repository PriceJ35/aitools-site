What Is Runway?

Runway is a professional AI video generation and editing platform. Gen-4, its latest model, produces cinematic quality videos from text descriptions or images. It offers precise camera control, character consistency across frames, and integrates with professional video editing workflows. Used by filmmakers, content creators, and marketing teams worldwide.

Key Features

  • Gen-4 Video Model — Cinema-quality video generation with consistent characters and smooth motion
  • Motion Brush — Precisely control which elements move and how
  • Camera Controls — Define camera angles, movements, and transitions
  • Image-to-Video — Animate static images into dynamic video clips
  • Text-to-Video — Generate video from detailed text descriptions
  • AI Editing Suite — Remove backgrounds, expand frames, slow motion, and more
