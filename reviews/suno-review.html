What Is Suno?

Suno is an AI music generation platform that creates complete, production-quality songs from text descriptions. It generates vocals, instruments, lyrics, and arrangements in various genres and styles. Whether you need background music for videos, jingles for ads, or full songs, Suno produces impressive results in seconds. It supports multiple languages, genres, and custom lyrics.

Key Features

  • Text to Song — Generate complete songs with vocals from text descriptions
  • Custom Lyrics — Write your own lyrics or let AI generate them
  • Genre Versatility — Pop, rock, hip-hop, jazz, classical, electronic, and more
  • Multi-language — Generate songs in 10+ languages
  • Audio Extension — Extend generated songs beyond initial clip length
  • Stem Separation — Separate vocals and instrumentals for mixing
